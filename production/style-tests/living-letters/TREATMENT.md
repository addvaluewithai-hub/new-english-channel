# Living Letters - treatment (17 s style test)

**Idea.** Type is the cast and the set. Youssef is a warm-brown "Y" (beard stroke, navy
jacket, serif feet), Mia a mustard "M" (ginger hair cap + ponytail, teal outfit). London is
the word LONDON standing as buildings: the second O is the London Eye, the dot of an "i" is
Big Ben's clock, a red "BUS" drives past, the clouds are "ooO". Spoken English is not a
caption - the words leave the speaker's mouth and land on the ground, which is the stage.

| t (s) | beat | picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.8 | "أول يوم ليّا في لندن…" | LONDON is typeset: letters spring up out of the ground. "Day 1" tag. Y walks in pulling his red suitcase; BUS passes. |
| 2.8-5.2 | "وكنت فاكر إن الإنجليزي بتاعي كويس." | He looks up, jumps (sparkles), then a smug thought chip: "English ✓". |
| 5.15-5.95 | transition | Camera dives into the London Eye; the school is already alive inside the ring (dolly-through, no cut). |
| 5.9-7.8 | "لحد ما قابلت ميا." | ENGLISH SCHOOL sign typesets letter by letter; Y rolls in; camera settles to a two-shot. |
| 7.3-7.7 | Mia appears | M drops in from above like a piece of type and lands with a squash and dust ticks. |
| 8.0-9.5 | "Hi! I'm Mia." | She waves; teal words pop from her mouth onto the stage. |
| 9.9-13.6 | "Hello! I... I Youssef." | Wave + hop on Hello!; "I..." trembles, dots appear one by one, eyes dart; "I" and "Youssef." land with a visible empty gap. A dashed violet slot fades into the gap. |
| 14.6-15.4 | "Sorry?" | Mia tilts, one brow up; "Sorry?" lands, its "?" wobbles. |
| 15.3-17.0 | embarrassed | Push-in to a close-up: Y shrinks, blush, sweat drop, wobbly mouth. The violet word "am" peeks in from the right edge with eyes on the slot - next episode's hook. Fade on the last 0.35 s. |

Teaching hook: the grammar mistake is visible as negative space on the word stage, and the
missing word is a character waiting in the wings.
