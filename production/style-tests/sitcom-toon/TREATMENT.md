# Treatment — "First Day" (Sitcom Toon style test, 17 s)

Style: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (flat adult-animation sitcom look: thick boiling purple-black line, flat fills with hard shadow shapes, big white eyes with tiny pupils, elastic mouths, acting on twos). No sci-fi content: an ordinary bright London street. Picture only; the voice track is fixed (`../audio/test_audio.wav`), timing from `../timeline.json`.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Single journey, wide to tight** — wide establishing street, Youssef walks in; two-shot at the door; reaction close-up. Classic sitcom coverage, clearest for learners.
2. **Cold open on the door** — start on the school door, pull out to reveal the street and Youssef. Stronger hook but the narration (which is about arriving) fights the image.
3. **Diary page frame** — street drawn inside a notebook page that "comes alive". Cute, but the series would be tied to a device that slows every episode.

**Choice: 1**, with a sitcom twist: the "suspiciously normal world" — a flat, bright, deliberately pleasant palette — and the comedy lives in the duo contrast (Mia breezy and confident, Youssef rehearsed and over-prepared). Ending: the cold-pause push-in on his frozen embarrassed grin (the style's "dead-air" move), the native move at the peak.

## Logline and arc
Youssef arrives in London sure of his English; one missing "am" and a single "Sorry?" turn him into a sweating tomato.
Setup (0–7.7 s arrival, confident-nervous) → turn (Mia's "Sorry?", 14.5 s) → ending (frozen grin, sweat, blush, slow push-in, fade).

## Benchmark
Adult-animation TV sitcoms (dialogue-driven, held poses, reaction close-ups). Learn: held poses with tiny changes, hard cuts to reaction close-ups, flat colour bands for sky. Don't take: any character design, colour combo, catchphrase, logo.

## Palette (the "suspiciously normal" world)
Sky: two flat bands of pale cornflower blue. Brick terraces: warm terracotta + cream trim, sage and navy doors. Bus: pillar-box red. School: cream stone with a teal door (Mia's skirt matches the school, Youssef's suitcase matches the bus). Line #2a1f33, eye white constant.
