# Youssef's Diary: "Day 1" (Watercolor Brush style test, 17 s)

Style: Watercolor Brush (`styles/watercolor/STYLE.md`, kept as is). Voice track: the supplied `test_audio.wav`, unchanged.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One continuous handscroll.** A single painted London street, the camera dollies along it as it paints itself in. Youssef walks through it to the school, where Mia paints in at the door. The camera settles into a locked two-shot and then leans in on Youssef. No cuts.
2. **Three journal entries.** Each shot is its own sheet that slides in over the last one (page turns): the street, the doorway, Youssef's face.
3. **Specimen plate.** A locked page with Youssef as the "specimen" and notes around him ("red suitcase", "nervous"). London paints in behind him.

**Chosen: 1, plus the diary device from 3.** Youssef narrates a diary, so the whole film is a single page of his journal being painted. The handscroll gives the establishing shot. The page then stops moving for the dialogue, and a slow push gives the close shot. Moving in one sheet with no cuts follows the style's camera grammar ("cutting is rare").

## Logline and arc
On his first day in London, Youssef is sure his English is ready, until a friendly "Hi! I'm Mia." meets "Hello! I... I Youssef."
Setup: a bright painted street, excitement. Turn: the greeting, his missing "am". Ending: "Sorry?" leaves him blushing. The diary hand puts a small vermilion caret with "am" into his line, which is the lesson hook. The page then mists to paper at the edges.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Travel-sketchbook watercolours and field-journal plates. **Learn:** generous paper margins, unpainted paper as the light, hand-written labels with leader lines, and a continuous panorama. **Don't take:** any specific artwork, layout or character design.

## Beat sheet
- 0.0: paper; the brush-edge wipe starts painting the street. 0.6–7.7: diary subtitle lines (Arabic, RTL, bottom).
- 1.0: a hand note "Day 1 · London" is written top left. 2–6: a red bus crosses the street.
- 3–7: Youssef walks right, pulling the red suitcase (the accent colour).
- 7.4: Mia paints in at the door. 8.0: "Hi! I'm Mia." note with a leader line; she waves.
- 9.9: "Hello! I... I Youssef." Youssef talks nervously, scratching his neck.
- 14.5: "Sorry?" Mia tilts her head with a brow raised. 14.6: Youssef blushes (a rose wash) and a sweat drop paints in.
- 15.9: a vermilion caret with "am" is written into his note. 16.5–17: mist from the edges.
