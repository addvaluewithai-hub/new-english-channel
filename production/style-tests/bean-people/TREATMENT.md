# "First day" — Bean People treatment (17 s style test)

**Idea.** Youssef's diary as a flat, sunny picture-book London. People are beans: one silhouette, one
colour band of clothing, a signature accessory. Acting is carried by squash/stretch, lean, the face
sliding round the bean, brows and a single mouth line — readable at phone size.
