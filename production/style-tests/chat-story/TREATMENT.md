# TREATMENT — "Youssef's Phone" style test (17 s)

The diary *is* his phone. Everything the audience sees is a screen they already know how to read.

| time | device | camera |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.9 | Camera app: flat London street (red bus, ENGLISH SCHOOL). Shutter + flash. | Establishing: whole phone rises into a warm paper room (z 0.74). |
| 0.8–1.3 | The photo shrinks into a Notes card ("يومياتي", Day 1 · London, location chip). | Slow push begins. |
| 0.95–7.4 | Voice dictation: each Arabic sentence appears as grey interim text, words turn ink as he says them (RTL, 36 px). Mic waveform pulses with his voice. "ميا" turns teal, a "Mia" contact chip pops (7.1). | Push into the note (→ z 1.2). |
| 7.35–7.95 | The phone turns to landscape → **Face-to-face** mode (LIVE). Mia's tile connects, "You met Mia · nearby". | Pull back, then the two-shot (z 1.6): both portrait tiles + transcript between them. |
| 8.0–9.5 | Mia's bubble: typing dots, then "Hi! I'm Mia." word by word on her spoken words; teal speaking ring + mouth. | Two-shot. |
| 9.9–13.4 | Youssef goes nervous (expression swap, sweat drop). His bubble types "Hello! I... I Youssef." on the spoken words; the typing dots *freeze* in his pauses (hesitation). | Slow drift toward Youssef. |
| 13.45–14.3 | "Heard" ticks → "Seen" (teal). He smiles, relieved. | Back to the even two-shot. |
| 14.5–15.3 | Mia swaps to puzzled (brow up, head tilt), typing dots, "Sorry?". Teal "?" reaction chip lands on his bubble. | Two-shot. |
| 15.3–17.0 | Youssef → embarrassed (closed eyes, blush, wobbly mouth, sweat); red spell-check squiggle draws under "I Youssef."; he starts typing… and stops. Room warms to blush pink. | Push into Youssef's tile (z 2.4). Fade to room colour 16.5–17. |

Reusable per episode: notes/dictation, camera/photo card, face-to-face transcript, avatars with 4–5
expressions, reaction chips, squiggle (the series' recurring "mistake marker" — the fix can come later in
the lesson). New per episode: one flat photo illustration, the timeline (lines + word times), camera keys.
